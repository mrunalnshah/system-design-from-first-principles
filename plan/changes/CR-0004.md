# CR-0004: Two-tier licence policy for the dependency audit (TOOL-14)
Status: approved (2026-11-26) · Date: 2026-11-26 · Raised in: T-00.10 (R-04)

## Why
plan/tools.md#tool-14-dependency-audit allows only MIT, ISC, BSD-2/3, Apache-2.0, OFL-1.1, 0BSD and BlueOak-1.0.0.
The real lockfile (984 entries) has 33 packages outside that list, all build or development tools whose code never
reaches readers:
- MPL-2.0: lightningcss (Vite's CSS minifier, 12 platform builds), axe-core and @axe-core/playwright (dev)
- LGPL-3.0-or-later, alone or with Apache-2.0/MIT: sharp's libvips binaries (Astro's image tooling, optional per platform)
- PSF-2.0: argparse (dev) · CC0-1.0: mdn-data · (MIT OR CC0-1.0): type-fest (dev) · FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0: sentry (dev, via Lighthouse)
What readers download comes only from KaTeX, @panzoom/panzoom and the @fontsource-variable fonts (MIT / OFL-1.1).
Applied as written, the audit would fail on every run for tools that carry no licence obligation to readers.

## What
MODIFIED plan/tools.md#tool-14-dependency-audit, "Does": the licence check has two tiers.
1. **Shipped to readers** (KaTeX, @panzoom/panzoom, @fontsource-variable/* and everything they depend on): permissive only:
   MIT, ISC, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0, OFL-1.1, 0BSD, BlueOak-1.0.0, CC0-1.0.
2. **Build and development tools** (everything else): the same, plus MPL-2.0, LGPL-3.0-or-later, PSF-2.0 and
   FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0 (each kept in a list with its reason).
Anything else (GPL, AGPL, SSPL, unknown or missing licence) fails in either tier. TOOL-14.AC2 (GPL-3.0 fails) still holds.

## Impact
plan/tools.md only; no requirement, point or round changes. Adding a licence to either list later needs a line in
specs/stack.md explaining why.

## Decision
Approved by the user on 2026-11-26 and merged into plan/tools.md.
