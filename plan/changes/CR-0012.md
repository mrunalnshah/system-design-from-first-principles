# CR-0012: Diagram quality and responsiveness are measured by layout checks, not screenshot baselines
Status: approved (2026-11-27, the author in R-15); merged into specs/product.md and specs/standards.md · Date: 2026-11-27 · Raised by: T-04.04's review (R-15)

## Why
NFR-010 and NFR-011 say they're measured by "screenshot tests", and specs/standards.md#testing says "screenshot
baselines are approved at the end of each screen task". No baseline was ever made: none is in the repository.
Playwright's baselines are per platform (`name-win32.png`, `name-linux.png`) and the fonts render differently on
each, so baselines made on the author's Windows computer would be missing on CI's Linux runner, and every push would
fail. Making Linux ones needs Docker or a CI run that uploads them, and then every intentional change to a screen means
regenerating and re-approving them.

What the two NFRs promise is a property of the layout, which T-04.04's tests check directly on every push:
- NFR-010: every diagram is the theme's SVG (so it stays crisp at any zoom), at most 900 px wide, at least as wide as
  its column, inside the screen at 320 px, and still all of that at 200% zoom (1280 px laid out at 640).
- NFR-011: no sideways scroll on any screen at 9 widths from 320 to 1920 px, light and dark, JavaScript on and off.
Each run also attaches screenshots to the test report for a person to look at.

## What
MODIFIED `specs/product.md` NFR-010, How it's measured: "Layout checks at 320, 768 and 1280 px in light and dark (the
theme's SVG, width up to 900 px and at least the column's, inside the screen, and at 200% zoom), with screenshots
attached to the report".

MODIFIED `specs/product.md` NFR-011, How it's measured: "An overflow assertion on every screen at 9 widths from 320
to 1920 px, with screenshots attached to the report".

MODIFIED `specs/standards.md#testing`: "screenshot baselines are approved at the end of each screen task" becomes
"screenshots are attached to the report and looked at against the design preview at the end of each screen task;
pixel baselines aren't kept (CR-0012: they differ by platform)".

No task, point or round changes. T-04.04's tests already do this (`.site/tests/e2e/journeys.spec.ts`).

## Impact
`<tools> impact specs/product.md specs/standards.md`: the two NFR rows and one testing rule; no code. The design preview
comparison at the end of each screen task stays as it is.

## Options considered
1. **Proposed:** layout checks plus attached screenshots (this CR).
2. Keep the measure: generate Linux baselines (Playwright's Docker image, or a CI job uploading them), commit them,
   and compare on every push. More upkeep: any intended visual change needs new baselines approved.
